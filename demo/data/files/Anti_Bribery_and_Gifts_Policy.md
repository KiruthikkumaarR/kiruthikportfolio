# Anti-Bribery and Gifts Policy

## Gifts and hospitality

Gifts above EUR 75 must not be accepted. Hospitality above EUR 150 per person requires prior approval by the Compliance Officer.

## Third parties

Agents and distributors are screened before appointment and every two years thereafter.

## Reporting

Concerns can be raised anonymously through the whistleblowing hotline, available 24 hours a day.
