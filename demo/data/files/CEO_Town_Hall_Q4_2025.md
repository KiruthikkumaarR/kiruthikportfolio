# CEO Town Hall - Q4 2025 summary

Presented by Maria Peltoniemi on 11 December 2025.

## Results

FY2025 revenue grew 6.3% to EUR 412.6 million and safety performance improved to an LTIFR of 0.62.

## What's next

Our 2026 priorities are SmartFlow 2.0, the HX-700 launch and reducing attrition. Every employee will receive a one-off recognition payment of EUR 500 in January 2026.

## Q&A highlights

Asked about remote work, the CEO confirmed the 3-days-per-week policy remains unchanged in 2026.
