# Company Overview

Halvorsen Industrial Systems (HIS) designs and manufactures centrifugal pumps, pump controllers and digital monitoring services for water, energy and industrial customers across Europe.

## Key facts

- Founded: 1962 in Tampere, Finland
- Group revenue FY2025: EUR 412.6 million
- Employees: about 3,940 (including production staff)
- Manufacturing sites: Tampere Works, Gdansk Assembly, Porto Foundry, Brno Machining, Lyon Systems, Bilbao Plant, Graz Electronics, Aarhus Service Centre
- Chief Executive Officer: Maria Peltoniemi
